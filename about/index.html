About

Nick Ficara

I'm a maker with experience in software, hardware, and almost everything in between. I'm also an amateur radio operator under the callsign VA3NDF and the operator of AS214681, a small autonomous system I run for the fun of learning how the internet fits together.

Most of what ends up here falls into two piles. Writing covers software, radio, homelabbing, and networking. Builds covers the physical side: laser cutting, machining, PCB work, leather, and whatever else my little shop allows.

You can find me on my personal site, LinkedIn, or by email at nick@nicholasficara.dev. There's also a puzzle on this site. If you crack it, I'd genuinely like to hear from you!
